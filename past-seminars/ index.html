    From Gradients to GPTs: The Mathematics Behind Large Language Models

    Ilyes Hamdi

    Abstract

    Large language models went from autocompleting sentences to solving olympiad problems in under a decade, yet the mathematics behind them is surprisingly simple. This talk follows the training pipeline step by step: pretraining on next-token prediction, supervised fine-tuning to follow instructions and answer questions, and reinforcement-learning methods that align models with human preferences and teach them to reason. Along the way we revisit the key ideas behind this progress, from the scaling bet that shaped early OpenAI to the efficiency and reasoning breakthroughs introduced by DeepSeek. At each stage we isolate the core mathematical principle that makes it work and show why it matters in practice. .
