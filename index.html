FreeStyle Math

An online research seminar for mathematics that doesn’t fit neatly into one box.

Each session, a speaker presents work in progress, a surprising connection between fields, or an open problem they can’t stop thinking about. Talks are aimed at a broad research audience, with plenty of time for questions and a relaxed discussion afterwards.

Why “FreeStyle”?

Most seminars are organized around a single field. This one is organized around curiosity. Speakers are free to choose the topic, the level of polish and the format, whether that means a blackboard-style derivation, a computer demo or a list of conjectures.
