Research

The seminar has no fixed field, but some themes keep coming back.

Geometry and topology

Shapes, spaces and their invariants, from low-dimensional topology to discrete geometry.

Number theory

Primes, Diophantine equations and the structures that organize them.

Dynamical systems

How systems evolve over time, including chaos, ergodic theory and bifurcations.

Combinatorics

Counting, graphs and extremal problems, often with surprising links to other areas.

Mathematical physics

Problems where physical intuition and rigorous mathematics meet.

Computation and experiment

Computer-assisted proof, formal verification and experimental mathematics.
